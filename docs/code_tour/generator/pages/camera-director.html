01Big picture

Preset camera shots, a button bar to switch between them, and a mode that follows a car. Every shot is computed by ABACSceneBuilder.MakeShots from the lane coordinates, so moving a lane and rebuilding moves the cameras with it.

The six shots

All approach shots stand on one point in the middle of the junction and only turn and rise, like one camera on a mast. That is the camera the project proposes to install; the drone was only for collecting data.

02Moving the camera

The easing factor is k = 1 − e^(−Δt / moveTime × 3) per frame, using unscaled time, so the camera glides at the same pace at 1× and 10×. In follow mode it picks the car of the shot's lane nearest the shot's position (CarAgent.NearestInLane) and re-picks every 0.4 s.
