01Big picture

Menu ABAC > Rebuild Car Prefab, also run by every scene build. It turns the 3D model car_low_poly.fbx into Car.prefab, working everything out from the model instead of hard-coding it. Swapping the model file and pressing the menu again just works.

02The seven steps

  1. Strip the cameras and lights Blender leaves in the file, or every car would carry its own.
  2. Align the long axis with z, Unity's forward.
  3. Find the body: the largest part, and within it the material slot covering the most area, which is the only slot painted per lane.
  4. Face forward: find the reddest material slot (the tail lights) and turn the car 180° if they are at the front.
  5. Scale to exactly 4 m long, matching CarAgent.carLength.
  6. Ground it, so the wheels touch the road when spawned at CAR_Y = 0.5.
  7. Equip: collider, kinematic Rigidbody, the detection outline, RoiHighlight and CarAgent; then save the prefab.

Why a Rigidbody on a car moved by script. Unity fires trigger events only when one of the two colliders has a Rigidbody. Without it, ROIZone.OnTriggerEnter never fires and no car is ever counted (comment at line 121).

03The detection outline

The green box RoiHighlight shows is twelve thin opaque bars, sized from the car's own bounds plus 6 cm. A translucent solid box was tried first, but in a stopped queue you could see through one box into the next and the queue read as one green mass. Thin bars keep every car countable. The padding is kept tiny so two outlines never touch at the 2 m queue gap.
