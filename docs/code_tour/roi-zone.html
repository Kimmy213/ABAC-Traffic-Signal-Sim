01Big picture

A detection zone: a 100 m trigger box laid back from a stop line. It stands in for the camera the project proposes. The file has two jobs that must not mix. It is the controller's only input, a count, and it is also the experiment's measuring tape, each car's wait. The split between what the controller may use and what is measurement-only is the core idea of the file.

  • Track which cars are inside, with the time each entered (Unity trigger events).
  • Count: GetSampledCount(), the only method AdaptiveController may call.
  • Measure: when a car leaves, report its time inside to DataLogger, under the car's own lane name.
  • Show: the lane card on screen, the yellow gizmo in the editor, and the green box on cars inside.

SodaTest scans AdaptiveController.cs and fails if it ever calls one of the three tracker methods. That is the test that keeps the simulated controller installable with the hardware the project can afford.

What the snapshot sees

The grey line is the true number of cars in a zone at every moment. The blue dots are what Snapshot() reads, once per snapshotInterval. The arrival and departure times in this drawing are made up for illustration; the sampling rule is the code's. Widen the interval and watch the rising edge, which sets queueStart, arrive later.

03Entering and leaving

The wait recorded is the time from entering the zone to leaving it. Every waiting-time figure in the project, including the v5 result, is this number, averaged. Two consequences follow from the code:

  • A car is logged only when it leaves the zone. A car still inside when the run ends is not counted.
  • Waits are counted only inside the 100 m zone. When a queue is longer than the zone, the time spent queuing behind it is not in the figure. This matters most in an oversaturated scenario such as EveningPeak, where queues reach beyond the zone.

Triggers fire because every car carries a kinematic Rigidbody, added by CarPrefabBuilder. Trigger timing is frame-grained: about ±0.17 s per car at 10× speed, which the ScenarioManager comment judges small against the 2 s snapshot used when it was written.

04One zone, two movements

A2 (through) and Ax (turning in) share the outer eastbound lane, and a camera cannot tell which car will turn. So they share one zone, ROI_Ax, with alsoCountLanes = ["A2"]. The controller sees their total; the statistics still separate them by the car's own assignedLane.
