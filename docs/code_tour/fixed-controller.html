01Big picture

The baseline every result is measured against. It replays the junction's real fixed-time plan, three phases in a fixed order with fixed greens, and never looks at traffic. It must reproduce the real junction's problem, or the comparison with SODA means nothing.

  • Resolve the phase plan's lane names to signal indices once, and refuse to open two conflicting lanes together.
  • Cycle: green for each phase, then yellow and all-red only for the lanes the next phase does not keep.
  • Report its cycle length (106 s) to the control panel and the console.

02The plan: three phases, four signals

The plan comes from Kimmy timing the real signal on site, checked by Itim against video and against his stage table. It lives in LoadABACPreset, and the same three phases are saved in the scene:

PhaseGreen lanesGreenThen
P1 MainThroughA, A2, B40 sA and A2 go yellow + all-red; B stays green into P2
P2 EastRightTurnB, Bx20 sB and Bx go yellow + all-red together
P3 ABACExitCB31 sCB goes yellow + all-red

The scene holds exactly these three phases (read from SampleScene.unity at build time). The plan is deliberately left unbalanced: Bx runs at v/c 1.51 while A and B sit at 0.25. Moving 10 s from A to Bx would help, but then Fixed would no longer be the real junction (comment, lines 241-245).

Watch one cycle, and change it

Press Play to run the cycle on the junction plan. The timing is the one RunCycle produces. The sliders change the plan as if it were edited in the Inspector, and the cycle length follows GetCycleLength. Notice that B stays green straight through from P1 into P2, and that Ax may only turn while Bx is red.

03Setup: names to indices, with a safety check

Phases name lanes as strings. BuildPhaseCache turns them into signal indices once. It also drops any lane that would conflict with another lane in the same phase, so a typo in the Inspector cannot open two crossing streams together.

04The cycle

This is the same "only the lanes that must stop" rule that SODA's clearance uses, with the same 3 s yellow and 2 s all-red. The two controllers therefore pay clearance the same way, and differ only in when they change and what they open.

Both controllers sit on GameController and both start at Play. ForceStop and RestartCycle let ScenarioManager and the control panel stop one and restart the other cleanly:

05Cycle length

40 + 20 + 31 + 3 × (3 + 2) = 106 s. FixedCycleTest checks this. The overhead clip measured the real cycle at 107.17 s (TestCaseLibrary.Compare101); the code keeps 106 s, for the reason in the first issue below.

07Known issues and decisions

  • Do not changeOn 15 Sep an allRedOverride field was added to FixedPhase, and the scene YAML edited by hand to stretch the cycle to the measured 107.17 s. In Unity every signal then stayed red and Fixed stopped working in every case. The out-of-Unity tests passed throughout, because they build controllers in code and never read the scene. The change was reverted; the signal system is not to be modified.Working session of 15 Sep 2026; the change was never committed, so it is not in the repository history
  • DecisionBx = 20 s, not 21 or 23: a stopwatch gave exactly 20.0 s, while 23 s came from a different method.Comment, lines 234-236
