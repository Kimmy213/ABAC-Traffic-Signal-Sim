01Big picture

Menu ABAC > Build 4-Phase Scene. One press builds the whole junction: roads and markings, four lights, five stop lines, six detection zones, the turning paths, seven spawners, the car prefab, and every controller and panel, all wired together. It is idempotent: pressing it again reuses existing objects instead of duplicating them. Every coordinate comes from constants at the top of the file, taken from the real site.

02The coordinates

x runs east along Bangna-Trad Road, z runs north into ABAC, in metres. The junction plan on the overview page is drawn from these numbers.

The A2 merge constants matter more than they look. Itim counted all three through cars that entered the shared lane changing lanes about five car lengths before the turn, cutting in at the head of the queue. A2 therefore merges within 12 m of a point 20 m before the stop line. Merging further back would spread A2 cars through the queue: the same average, but frequent mild blocking instead of the rare, severe blocking actually observed.

03What one press builds

  1. Roads, ground, buildings, lane markings (solid centre lines, dashed lane dividers, nothing across the junction box)
  2. Four lights: Light_A (drives A and A2), Light_B, Light_Bx, Light_CB
  3. Stop lines for A, B, Bx, CB and Ax; Ax's is a real one, because Ax waits while Bx is green
  4. Six zones, 100 m long and 3 m wide; ROI_Ax also counts A2
  5. Turning paths on a 7 m arc for Ax, Bx, CB and CA, and the A2 merge path
  6. Seven spawners with direction, light, path and lane colour; Ax tied to the Bx light, inverted
  7. The car prefab, rebuilt from the 3D model and handed to every spawner
  8. IntersectionConfig preset, wired lane by lane; then the controllers, logger, batch manager, panels and camera

Lines 274, 282 and 291 call the three presets on every build. That is the third guard against the scene overriding the code: rebuilding the scene always rewrites the measured values.

04Camera shots

The shots are drawn on the CameraDirector page.

05Checking the scene

Menu ABAC > Validate Scene checks the lanes and spawners. Every signalised lane needs a stop line. A free lane must have neither a stop line nor a light, unless it is a yielding lane like Ax. No lane may be counted by two zones, which would log its waits twice.

06Every constant

All const, so the scene cannot override them; they only take effect when the scene is rebuilt. Changing one moves objects in the scene, and every batch after it runs on a different junction.
